01 · Study Overview

What the study is

A descriptive cross-sectional online survey study in 2026, designed to understand how the public in Saudi Arabia perceives natural hazards and what is needed for a trusted early warning.

Study design & instrument

Descriptive cross-sectional study — a 15-question online questionnaire (Q1–Q15): 4 demographic questions + 3 knowledge/attitude 5-point scales + 3 conditional/descriptive questions + 5 preparedness/needs questions. No behavioral measurement.

⚠ The sample is non-probabilistic — results must NOT be generalized to the whole Saudi population. They are exploratory evidence, not national statistics.

Collection & verification

  • ACollection window: 20 Oct – 20 Nov 2026 (32 days). Mean completion time 2:47.
  • BSampling: convenience/snowball sampling via social platforms. No probability frame.
  • CExtraction & verification: RASD_Combined_250_Records.pdf (218 pages) via pymupdf 1.23 with coordinates (x,y), wrapped-line merging and 250 ID matching (1–250). 10% manual random audit (25/250) + 19-field cross-check.
  • DData quality: 100% completeness for closed Q1–Q14; ~90.8% average for open Q15/Q16 (optional); no duplicates of ID; 3 contradiction flags retained; no silent imputation.

Statistical analysis — descriptively directed

Frequencies and proportions with 95% Wald CIs. Cross-tabs: χ² + Cramér's V (nominal) + Spearman ρ (ordinal). Multi-choice answers are computed as percentage of full sample. The study does NOT claim causality — correlation is not causation.

02 · Participants & Sample

Who are the respondents?

250 real responses from 9 regions and 4 age bands. The respondent-verified data — no synthetic records are claimed.

Geographic distribution (Q2)

69% of responses come from 3 regions (Makkah + Riyadh + Eastern). Jazan, Najran and Jouf are absent entirely.

Age distribution (Q1)

86% under 28 — a young sample. 40% under 18 with no parental consent documented.

03 · Key Findings

The strongest verified findings

Every figure carries its denominator (n=250). When the approved Evidence report and the raw data differ, the report value is presented as the approved figure, with the raw aggregate shown for reference.

ℹApproved report (Q13/Q11): 211/250 = 84.4%. Raw aggregate: 212/250 = 84.8%. Contrasts with Evidence figures are flagged here for transparency.

Scope of inference: non-probabilistic young sample — 86% under 28, 69% in 3 regions, Jazan/Najran/Jouf absent. Do NOT generalize to the whole Kingdom or to large age groups. The percentages are the verified dataset figures; approved-report figures that differ are flagged in the validation report — never silently substituted.
